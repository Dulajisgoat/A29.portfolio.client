// DossierThe Person Behind The Games

I'm Aditha Chamikara, an independent game developer driven by creativity, experimentation, and the ambition to turn ideas into interactive experiences.

I believe games should be more than just something people play. They should create worlds, tell stories, challenge players, and leave lasting impressions.

My work revolves around building immersive digital experiences, experimenting with 3D environments, designing gameplay systems, and bringing imaginative concepts to life through code.

From high-speed racing circuits to challenging survival arenas and open-road driving experiences, I enjoy exploring different genres and pushing the boundaries of what I can create.

Every project is an opportunity to learn something new, solve a different problem, and build something better than the last.

// Core LoopMy Development Philosophy

I approach game development with three things in mind.

01

Experience

A great game is built around how it feels to play. I focus on creating engaging mechanics, responsive controls, immersive environments, and experiences that keep players interested.

02

Experimentation

I enjoy exploring new ideas, testing different mechanics, and experimenting with interactive systems. Not every idea starts perfectly, but every experiment teaches me something valuable.

03

Execution

Ideas only become meaningful when they are brought to life. I enjoy taking concepts from an initial idea to a playable experience, refining the details along the way.

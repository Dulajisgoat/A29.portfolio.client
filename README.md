## SELECTED PROJECTS

### 01. DROPOFF
*A 3D Courier Driving Simulator*

Dropoff is a driving experience built around exploration, progression, and multiplayer interaction. Players can experience a courier-style world featuring a garage, multiple levels, different maps, and online party rooms.

The project combines driving mechanics with progression systems and social gameplay.

### 02. APEX 3D
*Formula Racing Experience*

Apex 3D brings the excitement of Formula-style racing into an interactive 3D environment. Featuring time trials, vehicle upgrades, leaderboards, and achievements, the experience focuses on speed, improvement, and competition.

### 03. VOREXA STRIKE
*Arcade First-Person Shooter*

Vorexa Strike is an arcade FPS experience built around wave-based combat and progression. Players face eight waves of enemies, encounter Vorexa Surge, experiment with augments, and eventually face the Warden boss.

It combines fast-paced gameplay with challenging encounters and upgrade-based mechanics.
